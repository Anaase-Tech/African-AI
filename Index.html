Our Story

ChatGPT doesn't understand Africa. It doesn't know our languages, our proverbs, our innovations. When asked about African content, it often gets it wrong or gives surface-level answers.

So we decided to change that.

African AI was built by a young African founder, Y.A B3rima, on a Samsung A04e phone in Ghana. Yes, a PHONE. No fancy equipment. No big funding. Just vision, determination, and belief in Africa.

In just 48 hours, we went from idea to working product. Now we're serving users across Africa and the diaspora, sharing authentic African knowledge that the world needs to hear.
